# Client Portal Guide

Welcome to the Trivexa Client Portal. Here you can track your project progress, approve deliverables, and manage invoices.

## 1. Accessing the Portal

We use **Magic Links** for secure password-less login.

1.  Check your email for an invitation from Trivexa.
2.  Click the secure link.
3.  You will be logged in automatically.
    - *Note: Links expire after 24 hours.*

## 2. Dashboard Overview

Your dashboard shows:
- **Active Projects**: Current status and next milestone.
- **Pending Actions**: Deliverables waiting for approval.
- **Outstanding Invoices**: Bills that need payment.

## 3. Managing Projects

### 3.1 Reviewing Deliverables
1.  Navigate to the **"Deliverables"** tab.
2.  Click on a file to preview it (PDF, Images).
3.  **Approve**: Validates the work.
4.  **Request Changes**: Opens a comment box to provide feedback.

## 4. Invoices & Payments

1.  Go to the **"Invoices"** tab.
2.  Click **"Pay Now"**.
3.  You will be redirected to our secure payment gateway (Stripe/PayPal).
4.  Once paid, a receipt is emailed to you instantly.
