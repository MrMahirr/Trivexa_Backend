# Service Level Agreement (SLA) Guidelines

This document defines the expected service levels for the Trivexa Agency Management System, applicable to both internal staff operations and external client portal access.

## 1. System Availability (Uptime)

We guarantee a **99.9% Uptime** during business hours (09:00 - 18:00 Local Time, Mon-Fri).

| Status | Definition |
|:---|:---|
| **Operational** | System is accessible and functioning normally. |
| **Degraded Performance** | System is slow but usable. |
| **Partial Outage** | A specific module (e.g., Invoicing) is down. |
| **Major Outage** | System is completely inaccessible. |

### Planned Maintenance
- Occurs outside business hours (e.g., Sunday 02:00 AM).
- Notified 48 hours in advance via email.

## 2. Support Ticket Prioritization

When a Client or Staff member submits a ticket via the Portal, it is assigned a priority.

| Priority | Definition | Response Time | Resolution Target |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Critical** | System down, data loss, or blocking financial operations. | < 1 Hour | 4 Hours |
| **High** | Major feature broken (e.g., Cannot create project). Workaround available. | < 4 Hours | 1 Business Day |
| **Medium** | Minor bug or difficulty using a feature. | < 1 Business Day | 3 Business Days |
| **Low** | Cosmetic issue, typo, or feature request. | < 3 Business Days | Next Release |

## 3. Client Portal SLA

For Agency Clients using the portal:
- **Quote Requests**: Acknowledged within 1 business day.
- **Invoices**: Sent at least 5 days before the Due Date.
- **Support**: Standard business hour support.

## 4. Escalation Matrix

If an SLA is breached:

1.  **Level 1**: Support Agent notifies User + updates ticket.
2.  **Level 2**: Account Manager is notified (If Resolution Target missed by 50%).
3.  **Level 3**: Agency Director is notified (If Critical issue > 4 Hours).

## 5. Credits & Penalties (Optional)

In case of valid SLA breaches regarding Uptime:
- **< 99.0%**: 10% Discount on next month's retainer.
- **< 95.0%**: 25% Discount on next month's retainer.
