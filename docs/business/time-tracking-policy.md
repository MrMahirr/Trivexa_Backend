# Time Tracking Policy

Accurate time tracking is essential for project budgeting, client billing, and payroll.

## 1. Tracking Expectations

### 1.1 Who Must Track Time?
- **All Production Staff**: Developers, Designers, Project Managers.
- **Administrative Staff**: Only for client-billable work.

### 1.2 When to Track?
- **Real-time**: Ideally, start/stop timers as you work.
- **Daily**: At minimum, all time for the day must be logged by **10:00 AM the following morning**.
- **Weekly**: Timesheets lock for approval on **Mondays at 12:00 PM**.

## 2. Log Entry Standards

Every time entry must include:
1.  **Project & Task**: Select the specific task (e.g., "Homepage Design"). Avoid generic "General" buckets.
2.  **Duration**: Accurate to 15-minute increments.
3.  **Description**: Brief summary of work done.
    - *Bad:* "Working"
    - *Good:* "Created wireframes for login page"

## 3. Billable vs. Non-Billable

- **Billable**:
    - Coding, design, testing.
    - Client meetings and calls.
    - Project-specific research.
- **Non-Billable**:
    - Internal team meetings (unless project-specific).
    - Professional development / Training.
    - Administrative tasks (Filling out timesheets, HR forms).

## 4. Overtime Policy

- **Standard Week**: 40 Hours.
- **Overtime**: Must be pre-approved by a Manager.
- **Weekend Work**: Only for emergencies or tight deadlines, with prior approval.

## 5. Correction Process

- **Before Locking**: User can edit their own entries.
- **After Locking**: User must request an edit from their Project Manager or Admin.
